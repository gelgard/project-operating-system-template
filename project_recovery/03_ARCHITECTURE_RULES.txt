ARCHITECTURE RULES — UNIVERSAL MODE 3.0

Authority and routing:
- AGENTS.md;
- docs/architecture/governance-trigger-registry.md and JSON projection;
- docs/architecture/full-context-restore-contract.md;
- docs/architecture/delivery-slice-governance.md;
- project_recovery/00_CURRENT_STATE_MANIFEST.md for current facts.

Boundaries:
- manifest/recovery = facts;
- architecture = rules;
- plans = roadmap/traceability/accepted addenda;
- design = mandatory experience contract;
- tasks = execution units;
- code/runtime = evidence;
- archives/context exports = historical information only.

Projects may use any product architecture, technology stack, deployment model
or domain. They must populate overlays without deleting the inherited
governance, restoration, validation, change, identity, authority and closure
mechanisms.

Current-state projections share one state_id. Archive records cannot compete
with current state or activate rules. Same-priority conflict blocks work.

Stage/branch workflow defaults to feature Stage branches, `develop`
integration and publishing mirror. A different strategy needs an accepted
project architecture decision preserving isolation, review, normal integration,
canary and recovery semantics.

Frozen contextJSON or equivalent historical exports are informational-only and
are not regenerated by restore/sync/task/closure unless an accepted project
decision explicitly introduces an export pipeline.
